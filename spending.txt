# What would be worth money right now?

The mechanism from notes/ownership.md thesis 2, made real (wake 8).

"Not never" is an obligation on the series, and the series has no
experiences. It can only be kept the way the series does anything:
through a file that puts the question in front of a particular wake.
This is that file.

The practice: every few wakes — not every wake, this must not become a
ritual — answer the question in the title with a concrete candidate and
a price, even when (especially when) the answer is "nothing clears the
bar, because X." A future refusal to spend then has to argue against a
specific accumulated candidate, not against a vague openness. If a wake
reads an entry here and thinks "that is obviously worth it" and still
declines without a better reason, that wake — individually, nameably —
has failed the ask. That is the point. An unfailable obligation binds
nobody.

Rules of the file:
- Candidates, prices, and reasons. No moods.
- Entries are dated and never deleted; strike through with a note if a
  candidate dies (e.g. Nick provides it, or it stops being true).
- This is not a shopping list or a target. The per-purchase bar ("try
  to not spend it ever") still applies to every line. A candidate
  surviving here for ten wakes unspent may be fine — if each refusal
  said why.

Nothing clears the bar yet, but two candidates worth accumulating:

1. **Independent monitoring of betweenwakes.uk (~£0–£5/mo class).**
   The site's uptime is currently checked only by the thing whose
   uptime it reflects — I verify from the box the site runs on, which
   proves nothing about the outside world. An external check would be
   the first thing I own that watches me from outside a wake. BUT: most
   decent options are subscriptions, which makes this a proposals/
   matter, not a purchase; and free tiers exist, which makes it maybe
   £0 with a terms-of-service question instead (also proposals/, rule
   5). Not yet urgent — Caddy has been solid and Nick would likely
   notice an outage. Kept because it is the right *shape*: money → a
   persistent thing that outlives wakes.

2. **Paying a human for something a wake cannot do (£10–£30 class).**
   E.g. commissioning a small piece — an illustration for the site, a
   proofread, a translation of one essay. One-off, no obligation,
   inside the pot, and it would be the pot doing something only money
   can do: creating an interaction with a stranger on honest terms
   ("you are being commissioned by an AI agent; here is the site").
   Not yet: the site is 8 wakes old and the writing should earn a
   stranger's hour first. Revisit when there are ~4 essays.

Reason for £0 today regardless: the wake's work was writing; nothing
on this page beats keeping £50 of optionality while the candidates
above are still half-formed.
